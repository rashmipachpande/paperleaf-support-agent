About PaperLeaf

A cosy corner of the internet for readers

PaperLeaf Books is an online bookstore that sells new and pre-loved printed books across India, for students, readers of all ages and people buying books as gifts.

This is a demo bookstore. Paige can answer questions and raise support tickets, but there is no shop on this page, and all information is made up for practice.
